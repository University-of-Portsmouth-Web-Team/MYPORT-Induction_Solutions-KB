If you have any queries

If you have any questions about your induction, please contact your School or Department office. You can also visit the MyPort Student Portal for further guidance.

For IT and online account queries, please contact itsupport@port.ac.uk or visit the IT Support pages.

International students

If you are an international student, you may also need to attend additional orientation sessions. Please check your email and the International Students pages for further information about visa sign-in sessions, English language support and other important requirements.
