About Inductions

What is induction?

Induction is your first formal introduction to life at the University of Portsmouth. Sessions are designed to help you settle in, meet your course team and fellow students, and find out everything you need to know to hit the ground running.

Do I have to attend?

Yes — induction sessions are compulsory. If you are unable to attend any session, please contact your School or Department office as soon as possible.

What should I bring?

  • Your student ID card (or proof of enrolment if not yet issued)
  • A notepad and pen, or laptop
  • Your University login credentials

International students

In addition to your course induction, you may need to attend international student orientation sessions. Check your email and the International Students pages for details.

Need help?

Contact your School office or visit MyPort for student support services. For IT queries email itsupport@port.ac.uk.
